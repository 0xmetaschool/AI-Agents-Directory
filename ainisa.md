# Ainisa

**Description**: Ainisa is an AI receptionist for businesses that automatically answers customer questions, captures leads, and books appointments 24/7 across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Telegram, and websites. Businesses can train their AI agents with their own knowledge and connect actions to automate real customer workflows without writing code.

**Features**:
- AI receptionist for customer support, lead qualification, and appointment booking
- Knowledge-based AI agents trained on business information, FAQs, services, and products
- Multi-channel deployment across WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Telegram, and websites
- AI actions for bookings, lead generation, CRM workflows, and custom API integrations
