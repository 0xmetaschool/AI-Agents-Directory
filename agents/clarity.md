# Clarity

**Description**: Clarity is a Base mainnet x402 research and chat API for AI agents and builders. Discovery is free via /.well-known/x402; external Base USDC payers buy short research reports ($2) or OpenAI-compatible chat ($0.001) without host-wallet funding.

**Features**:
- Free x402 discovery manifest and public OpenAPI for agent-native lookup.
- Paid short research reports settled in USDC on Base ($2 per report).
- OpenAI-compatible chat completions at $0.001 USDC per call.
- Public agent-tools.cloud card plus Funnel buy.md for humans and agents.
