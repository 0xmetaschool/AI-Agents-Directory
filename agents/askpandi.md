# AskPandi

**Description**: AskPandi is an agent that searches for you and gives you accurate answers from multiple sources, so you save time. It also creates a discovery feed for your own interests, making searching and learning new things joyful.

**Features**:
- Get answers instead of blue links. 
- Discovery feed with content created for you and your own unique interests.
- Upload and search your own data library. 
- Share your favourite answers to the Pandipedia so you help others too.
