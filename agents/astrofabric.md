# AstroFabric

**Description**: AstroFabric provides agentic AI for business intelligence. Autonomous agents discover companies and contacts, verify and enrich records, monitor business signals, score account fit, and deliver structured lists and audiences into connected systems.

**Features**:
- Company and contact discovery, verification and enrichment
- Business signals, account scoring and durable lists
- Hosted MCP server and REST API with workspace access controls
- Credit limits, evidence and configurable approval controls for external writes
