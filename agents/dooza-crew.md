# Dooza Crew

**Description**: Dooza Crew is an AI employee workspace for small businesses where multiple specialized AI agents work in a shared group chat, use a real browser and computer tools, handle phone calls and SMS, and pause for human approval before sending external emails or payments.

**Features**:
- Shared workspace where AI agents collaborate alongside human team members
- Autonomous browser, email, SMS, and voice-call task execution
- Built-in human approval gates and hard usage caps
- Free to try in the Dooza Crew app with no credit card required
