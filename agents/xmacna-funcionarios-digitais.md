# XMACNA Funcionarios Digitais

**Description**: XMACNA Funcionarios Digitais with IA are AI agents designed to execute business workflows such as lead qualification, WhatsApp follow-up, CRM updates, and human handoff. The public package shows how to model a Funcionario Digital with measurable outcomes, guardrails, structured CRM fields, and responsible rollout practices for teams adopting Inteligencia Artificial.

**Features**:
- AI employee workflow design for lead qualification and follow-up.
- WhatsApp and CRM handoff patterns with structured fields.
- Guardrails for human approval, auditability, and rollout risk.
- Public Claude Code agent and installable repository.
